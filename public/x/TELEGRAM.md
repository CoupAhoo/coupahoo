—— MECHANICS ——

You roll 3d6. That stack is your ship.

Pips = hit points.
Dice count = guns. More crates, more shots.
Empty deck = you sink.

If the pips sum to 13 the hull is cursed. The first ball of every volley goes overboard. Thirteen is not a score. It is a hole.

Combat dice are 0 / 0 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2. Roll a volley, keep or reroll, then click their cargo.

Between fights: merchants, shipwrights, sailors in dinghies, storms, free crates in the drink. Gold hires crew.

Gunner — first shot each volley +1.
Carpenter — after a win, patches the weakest cargo +1.
Lookout — enemy wastes their first shot.
Cook — +3 gold every ship you sink.

Plate a crate and it only takes 1 damage. Take the fleet one sloop at a time. Nine encounters. Last one is the flagship.
