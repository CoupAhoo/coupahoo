MECHANICS

Roll 3d6. That stack is your ship.
Pips = hit points. Dice count = guns. Empty deck sinks.

Sum to 13 and you are cursed. The first ball of every volley goes overboard. Thirteen is not a score. It is a hole.

Combat dice 0 / 0 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 2. Keep or reroll, then click their cargo. Plate a crate and it only takes 1.

Between fights: merchants, shipwrights, sailors in dinghies, storms. Gold hires crew.

Gunner — first shot +1.
Carpenter — patches the weakest crate after a win.
Lookout — they waste their first shot.
Cook — +3 gold per sink.

Nine encounters. Last one is the flagship.
