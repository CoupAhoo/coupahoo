# Coup Ahoo

Dice are your hull. Thirteen sinks you. Take the fleet.

Play at [coupahoo.lol](https://coupahoo.lol) · `$AHOO` on Robinhood Chain (4663).

## Play

Cargo dice are hit points and guns. Keep or roll your volley, then shoot their crates. If the pips sum to 13 you are cursed — the first shot of every volley goes overboard. Events between fights. Best logged run of each epoch can take The Chest.

## Token

LetsCash launch. 3% trade tax (0.3% platform, 2.7% The Chest). Split inside The Chest: 70% prize / 20% drip / 10% cabin.

CA posts at launch.
